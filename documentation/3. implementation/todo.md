| W13 | 🐞 **BUG — a broad/catch-all `RetentionPolicy` purges readings a more specific policy exists to protect, contradicting this package's own documented contract.** `RetentionPolicy.AssetType`'s own doc comment (`telemetry.go`) says an empty `AssetType` "applies to every asset type not covered by a more specific policy" — but `Sweep`/`PurgeBefore` (`retention.go`, `memory_telemetry.go`) implement no precedence at all: each registered policy is applied to the whole reading set independently, with `PurgeBefore(assetType="", ...)` matching *every* asset type and `PurgeBefore(metricName="", ...)` matching *every* metric, regardless of whether a more specific policy also names that (AssetType, MetricName) pair. Demonstrated live: register a specific policy (`AssetType: pipeline, MetricName: pressure_psi, RetentionDays: 365`) meant to keep a year of pressure readings, and a catch-all policy (`AssetType: "", MetricName: "", RetentionDays: 10`) meant to apply only to what the specific policy doesn't cover; record one `pipeline`/`pressure_psi` reading 40 days old (well within the 365-day retention, past the catch-all's 10 days); run `Sweep` — it reports `purged: 1`, and a follow-up `Query` for that asset returns zero readings. The reading the specific policy exists to protect for 365 days is gone after 40. The identical shape recurs one level up: an asset-type-wide policy (`AssetType: pipeline, MetricName: ""`) equally overrides a fully-specific policy scoped to one metric of that same type — same root cause (no most-specific-wins logic anywhere in `Sweep`), not a second bug. Defect location: `retention.go`'s `Sweep` (or `PurgeBefore`'s callers) need to exclude, from a broader policy's purge, any reading that a more specific *registered* policy already covers — the "not covered by a more specific policy" clause `telemetry.go`'s doc comment promises but nothing implements.
